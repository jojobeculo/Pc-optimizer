FrameCore — important information before installing

FrameCore is free and open-source software (MIT licence) that analyses and optimises Windows 11 for gaming.

What the installer does:
  • copies the application to the chosen folder (Program Files by default);
  • creates the requested shortcuts and an "Installed apps" entry for uninstalling;
  • changes NO Windows setting and installs no driver, service, scheduled task or third-party software.

What the application does:
  • runs without administrator rights for analysis; asks for elevation (UAC) only to apply a change you approved;
  • records the exact state of every setting before changing it and can restore everything (item by item or all at once);
  • offers to create a Windows restore point before changes;
  • never disables Microsoft Defender, the firewall, Windows Update, Secure Boot, the TPM or VBS/HVCI;
  • collects no data and sends nothing over the Internet without your explicit consent (no telemetry).

Known limitations:
  • the executable is not code-signed (no signing certificate was provided): Windows SmartScreen may show an "Unknown publisher" warning;
  • GPU overclocking relies on the official NVIDIA (NVML) and AMD (ADLX) interfaces; any overclock carries a risk of instability. CPU tuning and XMP/EXPO profiles are set in the BIOS: FrameCore gives guidance and never writes to the BIOS;
  • performance gains depend on your hardware; FrameCore measures them instead of promising them.

Your data (hardware profile, backups, history, benchmarks) is stored only on this PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FrameCore. It is never part of the installer.
